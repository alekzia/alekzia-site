Work About

Drive a Ford (Maverick and Bronco Sport)

Client — FORD
Agency — Wieden + Kennedy
Role — Copywriter

Too many people believe that an inexpensive vehicle has to be a milquetoast four-door sedan. The problem is, Ford doesn’t make four door sedans anymore. Instead, their “entry-level” vehicles, the Maverick and Bronco Sport, help their drivers do more of what they love. Getting outside, transporting gear, etc.

What started as a “value campaign” became a series of beautiful films that showcase these vehicles’ strengths and how they fit into drivers’ lives.
